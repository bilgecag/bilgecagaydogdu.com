fig. 01 — activity-space relocation after a shock

Displacement detection

A novel activity space approach to discover displacement patterns

EPJ Data Science · 2025

Rather than tracking single locations, this method models each person's whole activity space and detects when it relocates. Applied to the 2023 Turkiye and Syria earthquakes to identify who was displaced, when, and for how long.

Multi-source measurement

Combining Twitter and mobile phone data to observe border-rush

J. Computational Social Science · 2025

When the Turkish-European border opened in 2020, two very different data sources both registered the movement. Cross-validating them shows what each one sees, what each one misses, and how they can be read together.

Analyzing international airtime top-up transfers for migration and mobility

When someone abroad tops up a phone back home, that transaction is a trace of a social tie. Across 220 million transfers, those traces sketch migration corridors that conventional statistics struggle to see.
